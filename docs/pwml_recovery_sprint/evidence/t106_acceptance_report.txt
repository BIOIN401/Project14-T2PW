SEPARATED DENOMINATORS -- one question each
A rate here never shares a denominator with another rate. That is the whole point:
a retrieval problem and an exporter problem used to move the same number.
Note the two research rates. 'deliverable produced' is an OUTPUT rate -- the citation
report exists. 'semantically confirmed' is a CORRECTNESS rate. A deliverable built on
missing admission data, identity conflicts, unsupported reactions or orphaned
references is produced but not confirmed, and must never be quoted as a success.
