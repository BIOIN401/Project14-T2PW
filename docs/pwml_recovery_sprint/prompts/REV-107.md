### The trap most likely to be missed, stated plainly

**This card moves the guard in both directions at once, which makes it more dangerous than C-105,
not less.** Findings 1a and 1f **refuse more**; 1b, 1c, 1d and 1e **accept more**. The corpus can
absorb a large error in one direction if the other direction moved too, and the net row count will
look reasonable.

**B5 exists for exactly this and it requires you to run something.** Demand the two directions
separately. If the author reports only a net, that is a correction round on its own, regardless of
how good the rest of the work is.

### The second trap

**B4 is the one a reviewer skips because it looks like bookkeeping.** The 29-case battery is the
only instrument that caught C-105 round 1's 12-of-29 refusal, and it caught it because someone ran
it rather than reasoning about the regex. **Run it.**
