### The three guard rails — state them in code comments, and test each

1. The exemption applies **only** to terms on that case's own `forbidden_identifiers` — never to
   terms that are merely hard, absent, or unmatched. *"Not required as a coverage match" must never
   become "safe to omit anything."*
2. **No bare identifiers and no fabricated PWML** are introduced, in any form.
3. **Extracted-but-withheld entities remain visible in diagnostics.** Removing a term from the
   denominator must not remove it from the record.
