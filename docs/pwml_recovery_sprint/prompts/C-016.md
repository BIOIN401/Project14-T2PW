REQUIRED SEMANTICS -- PRODUCT_CONTRACT section 10 and D-005
  1. SIX termination reasons, NEVER conflated:
       retrieval_exhausted        the configured ladder ACTUALLY COMPLETED and
                                  produced no admissible new claims
       no_new_claims              retrieval completed but did not expand the graph
       budget_exhausted           another recovery step might have helped;
                                  wall-clock did not allow it
       operation_timeout          an individual external operation exceeded its
                                  deadline
       identical_empty_response   repeated extraction returned the same
                                  structurally empty response
       scientifically_unrecoverable
                                  evidence sources exhausted; no defensible core
     retrieval_exhausted may be claimed ONLY when the ladder actually completed.
     Claiming it when the budget ran out is the specific misreport D-005 forbids,
     because budget_exhausted counts as an OPERATIONAL failure in the
     pipeline-completion and strict-success denominators and the other does not.

  2. DEDUPLICATION IS AGAINST ALL CLAIMS EVER SEEN, not only admitted ones.
     PRODUCT_CONTRACT section 10 is explicit: otherwise judge-rejected claims
     recur every round and the loop never converges. Your policy must expose the
     seen-set semantics that make convergence provable.

  3. A rejected RAG claim must not be reintroduced by a later stage.

  4. The loop is BOUNDED and DEADLINE-AWARE. Bound both rounds and time.

IMPLEMENT
  1. A typed decision object: continue-or-stop, plus the exact reason when
     stopping, plus what was counted to reach that conclusion.
  2. A pure function from loop state to that decision. No I/O, no clock reads
     inside -- take the deadline and the current time as INPUTS so it is testable
     and so C-043 can drive it deterministically.
  3. The seen-set contract for deduplication against all claims ever seen.
  4. Explicit precedence when several stop conditions hold at once. Two reasons
     being simultaneously true is normal; the policy must be total and must
     prefer the reason that is most operationally honest. Document the order.
  5. Do not import the controller, the pipeline, or streamlit_app. Leaf module.
