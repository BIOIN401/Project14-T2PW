**P1 — the row actually reaches the matcher.** The seam at `semantic.py:1418` passed only `name`.
Prove by execution, not by reading, that the complete candidate row now reaches the predicate. A

**P2 — the exception cannot fire on name alone.** Construct a row named exactly `Unknown` on
PMC12444477 that fails `is_pathbank_unknown_protein(row)` on **each** of the four clauses

## 4. Standing review obligations

* **Run selections SPLIT, never combined.** A combined total hides a shift between two files. This
  caught a real move on C-100.
* **Check the guard that was REMOVED, not only the one added.** On C-099 that is exactly where the
  class of change went wrong.
* **Record your predictions in writing before you run.** Being wrong in writing is productive; being
  vague is not.
* **A control proves the instrument can report non-zero. It does not prove it is asking the right
  question.** Where a null result is load-bearing, the control must exercise **the same predicate**
  on a case known to match it. A case-sensitive `\bLpp\b` against a lowercase token passed its
  control and was still wrong.
* **Search for vacuous assertions.** An assertion that holds for every possible input is not a test.
* **Inspect moved baselines even outside SMOKE.** Three times this sprint a gate-invisible file hid a
  real change. `test_protein_export_policy.py` is in **neither SMOKE nor Chunk D**.
* **Verify the author did not commit a wrong measurement beside the corrected one silently** — and
  if they disclosed one, say so approvingly. Two authors did that this sprint and it was right.
* **Distinguish "this card passes" from "the wider finding is closed."** C-101 does not close F-132;
  that is C-102.

## 5. Environment traps that have voided reviewer jobs before

* **Exported `PATHBANK_DB_*` cannot hide the database.** `src/t2pw/llm/client.py:22` calls
  `load_dotenv(..., override=True)` and re-applies `.env` over your exported values. **Only
  physically renaming `.env` works**, with a `trap … EXIT`. A reviewer voided two jobs discovering
  this.
* **An agent worktree may have no `.env` at all** — the opposite hazard, and it makes a green base
  leg green for the wrong reason. `C:/t/c101` has **no `.env` and no `.venv`**; I verified it.
  **State which state each of your trees is in.**
* **`git archive` exports have no `.git`**, so anything shelling out to `git ls-files` degenerates
  silently and four corpus tests skip. Use a **real git worktree**.
* **`cmd | tee log | head -N` truncates the log via SIGPIPE.** Redirect, then grep.
* **`bounded_run.py`'s report carries no child stdout.** Commit the probe *and* its log.
* **`pinned_pytest.py` exits 98** without `PYTHONPATH` at your tree. 98 is a measurement failure,
  never a test result.
