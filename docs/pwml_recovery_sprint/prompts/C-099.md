## 2. The defect, stated exactly

`hydrate_species_references` runs **earlier** in `map_payload` and resolves species per row, stamping
`species`, `species_name`, `organism`, `species_id`, `pathbank_species_id`, `taxonomy_id`,
`species_ref` and `mapping_meta.species_resolution` (`_stamp_entity_species`, `:3139`).

Later, `_apply_pathbank_unknown_enzyme_fallback` builds a one-protein functional wrapper and does:

```python
complex_row.update(
    {
        "name": name,
        "species": _PATHBANK_UNKNOWN_SPECIES_NAME,        # "Arabidopsis thaliana"
        "organism": _PATHBANK_UNKNOWN_SPECIES_NAME,
        "species_id": _PATHBANK_UNKNOWN_SPECIES_ID,       # 4
        "pathbank_species_id": _PATHBANK_UNKNOWN_SPECIES_ID,
        "generated": True,
        "generation_reason": "single_protein_pathwhiz_wrapper",
        "components": [component],
    }
)
```

`update()` overwrites **unconditionally**. It replaces four keys and leaves `species_name`,
`taxonomy_id`, `species_ref` and `mapping_meta.species_resolution` alone — which is why the measured
row is **internally contradictory**: `species: "Arabidopsis thaliana"` beside
`species_name: "Escherichia coli"`, `taxonomy_id: "562"` and an *E. coli* `species_ref` at confidence
1.0. A released payload carried it: `runs_verify/2026-08-04_1754/papers/PMC12856317/strict` shipped
`pathway.pwml` with *Arabidopsis* on a **human** ALAS2 wrapper.

**The clobber is orthogonal to placeholder status.** It is not the O-1 disagreement; it is a bug that
happened to live on the same rows.

## 4. Required behaviour

**If the wrapper row already carries an internally consistent, source-supported species, do not
overwrite it.** Otherwise behave **exactly as today**.

"Source-supported" means the row's `species_ref` / `mapping_meta.species_resolution` records a
species that came from evidence:

* **`explicit_entity_species`** — strongest, **preferred over everything**, and the source both pinned
  examples carry;
* `biological_state_species`.

> **AMENDED 2026-08-27, REV-099 Finding 1 — `single_pathway_species` was listed here and is RULED
> OUT.** It was the third member of this list when the card was dispatched. REV-099 argued from
> `_single_pathway_species_hint` (`map_ids.py:3236`), which returns a hint only when the payload
> declares **exactly one** species and then applies it to a row that never stated its own — that is
> **payload-scoped inference**, deterministic rather than an LLM's, but inference. The other two
> members are **entity-scoped**: one is the entity's own stated species, the other comes from the
> row's biological state.
>
> The counter-argument, recorded so nobody has to reconstruct it: C-099 does not *apply* species —
> `hydrate_species_references` already did — so declining an overwrite is arguably not "defaulting to
> the pathway organism". Ruled against on three grounds. The charter forbids defaulting missing
> species to the pathway's dominant organism, and this card must not become the precedent cited for
> pathway-scoped species surviving into a shipped wrapper. **Both pinned examples O-1 names carry
> `explicit_entity_species`** (see the line above), so the narrower set preserves everything the
> ruling asked for and loses nothing it named. And § 4 below says to leave wrappers not covered
> *"unchanged pending their own evidence classification"* — the `single_pathway_species` rows are
> exactly that population.
>
> Measured, corpus-wide over 24 roots: **71** generated single-protein wrappers, **6** carrying a
> resolved species ref, splitting **4** `explicit_entity_species` / **2** `single_pathway_species`
> (`evidence/orch711_wrapper_species_census.{py,log}`, confirmed independently by the orchestrator
> and the reviewer). So this moves **2** rows back to today's behaviour and **nothing regresses**.
>
> **Reverse in one line** if the product owner ever rules pathway-scoped species is evidence.

**Not source-supported, and therefore no change in behaviour:**

* `novel_species` (`status: "novel"`, name `"Unknown species"`, `confidence: 0.0`,
  `reason: "no_species_source"`) — this is the 25-wrapper case;

under `mapping_meta.species_resolution`. Preserving one and clobbering another is how the current
contradiction was born.
