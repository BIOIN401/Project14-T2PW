## 0. Why these three belong on one card

All three are the same failure: **the control plane told an agent something false, and nothing could
catch it.** A stale map said a module was missing when it was wired. A pinned citation pointed
seventeen lines above its own table. A reviewer's evidence was reachable only from a worktree nobody

plausible-but-wrong content, and evidence that exists only outside git history are all silent. That
is what makes them worth a card rather than three opportunistic edits — and the third one is the
reason the sprint nearly lost **72 reviewer reports and 94 probes**.

## 3. The reviewer-evidence route — the one that needs a check, not prose

**72 reviewer G11 reports and 94 probes were nearly lost this sprint**, because a reviewer's
evidence lived only in a worktree and the merge that accepted the review did not carry it.

Prose alone will not hold this. **Build a deterministic check.**

### Required outcome

A script — suggested `docs/pwml_recovery_sprint/evidence/reviewer_evidence_route.py` — that, given a
task id and a worktree path, **fails loudly** unless every piece of load-bearing reviewer evidence is
reachable from the integration branch. At minimum it must:

1. **Enumerate the reviewer's G11 reports** — `docs/pwml_recovery_sprint/evidence/g11/<TASK>/*.json`
   in the worktree.
2. **Enumerate the reviewer's probe sources and outputs** — `evidence/<task-stem>_*` sources and logs.
3. For each, decide **reachability from integration by CONTENT**, not by filename — hash the
   worktree file and look for that content in the integration branch's tree. A same-named file with
   different bytes is **not** reachable evidence, and that distinction is the whole point.
4. **Exit non-zero, listing every unreachable item**, so it can gate a merge.

Plus a test, `tests/test_c109_reviewer_evidence_route.py`, that exercises it against a fixture:
**a case where evidence is reachable and passes, and a case where it is missing and the check
fails.** A check that cannot fail is not a check — prove it fails.

Then record the obligation in the sprint process documents: **the orchestrator may not merge a
reviewed card, and may not release or clean a reviewer worktree, until this check passes.**
**No worktree is ever pruned regardless** — that rule is unchanged and absolute.

**Scope discipline:** this check is about *evidence reachability*. It is not a linter, not a commit
hook, and it must not attempt to judge whether the evidence is *good*.
