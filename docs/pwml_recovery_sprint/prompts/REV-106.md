### The trap most likely to be missed

**The author will be told by three separate documents — the handoff, F-151 and REV-104's own
correction — that this repair is "re-pin to 72".** It is not. `withheld` and
`with_matched_forbidden` are derived pins that sit *after* the census assert and have never
executed against the grown corpus. An author who trusts the prose over the measurement ships a
file that is still red, and a reviewer who reads the diff instead of running the file will not
catch it. **Run the file.**

### The second trap

**Making the harness "runnable" by deleting its baseline precondition satisfies the card's
headline and destroys its purpose.** The precondition is what makes every mutation result mean
something. A7 exists for this and it requires you to run something, not to read something.
