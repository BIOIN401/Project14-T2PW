## Why this card exists

An independent `pwml-bio-auditor` adjudicated a **`product_contract_violation`**: a PathBank match
renamed a compound *and* stamped its identifiers even when the pipeline's own gate had recorded the
match as failed. Measured blast radius: 24 of the 124 distinct compound names in committed
`runs/**/final_mapped.json`. Full defect statement, ruling and measured effect are in
**`DECISIONS.md` § D-028** — that is the authoritative text; this file does not restate it.
