## The crux, stated once

**This card can only fail in one direction that matters: by rewarding an empty result that was a
CASUALTY rather than a DECISION.**

Getting condition 3 wrong permissively converts **every timeout on a negative control into a
`PASS`** — and negative controls are exactly the papers where "nothing" looks normal. That would
lose the ability to see a crashed leg precisely where it is hardest to see. **It is worse than not
doing the card at all.**

Spend your effort there. The rest is bookkeeping by comparison.
