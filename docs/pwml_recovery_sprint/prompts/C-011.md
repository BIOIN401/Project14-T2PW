### Gap 1 — object sharing across the seam · PRIMARY OWNER: **C-030**

The merge record:

> `final_mapped is result["payload"]` is TAUTOLOGICAL. It reduces to
> `bool(result["payload"])` — measured **True 35 / False 4**, exactly tracking
> `quarantine_ok` — so a mutant returning `deepcopy(canonical_export_payload)` is NOT
> detected. … **C-030, C-050 and C-052 must not treat this field as a share guard.**

**Why C-030.** C-030 is the **earliest** future card that owns
`streamlit_app.py :: freeze_canonical_payload` (`MASTER_PLAN.md:358`), the seam whose
return establishes the relationship, and it is the first card to make the hash wiring
load-bearing on it. C-052 also owns that function (`:375`) but lands later and depends on
C-030.

**C-030 REQUIREMENTS — binding.**

1. **Capture the caller-owned payload reference BEFORE the seam runs** — the identity of
   the object the caller holds going in — and prove the intended object-sharing
   relationship **after** `freeze_canonical_payload` returns. An assertion whose truth
   value is a function of `bool(result["payload"])` alone does not satisfy this.
2. **Prove the assertion is non-vacuous by mutation**: a seam returning
   `deepcopy(canonical_export_payload)` (share -> copy) **must fail** the new assertion.
   The existing `final_mapped is result["payload"]` line survives that mutant and is
   therefore not sufficient; it may be kept, but it may not be counted as the guard.
3. Cover **both branches**: the 35 `quarantine_ok` legs and the 4 refusal legs. An
   assertion that is only reachable when a payload is truthy re-creates the tautology.
4. **Observable behaviour only.** Do not change what the seam returns, do not add a copy,
   a conversion or a validation to make the assertion easier, and do not alter the freeze
   order to expose the reference.

**Cross-references, not ownership.** **C-050** must not introduce a pre-freeze step that
re-binds or replaces the caller's payload object in a way that breaks the relationship
C-030 pins. **C-052** inherits C-030's assertion and must keep it passing when it enforces
the freeze; it must not re-implement it.
