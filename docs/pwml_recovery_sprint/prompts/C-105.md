## 1. The defect, measured

`runs_verify/2026-08-28_1816/papers/PMC13231680/research/final_mapped.json`,
`/processes/reactions/0`:

```json
"name":      "phthalylsulfacetamide decomposition to sulfacetamide",
"inputs":    ["phthalylsulfacetamide"],
"outputs":   ["sulfacetamide"],
"enzymes":   ["NDM-1"],
"modifiers": [{"entity": "NDM-1", "role": "inhibitor",
               "evidence": "PSA significantly inhibited NDM-1 enzyme activity"}],
"evidence":  "PSA is decomposed in the intestine, resulting in an antibacterial effect"
```

The frozen graph asserts that the **same protein both catalyses and inhibits the same reaction**.
The reaction's own `evidence` string names **no enzyme at all**. The paper's thesis is the
opposite relation: the compound inhibits the protein.

The audit stage says in writing why it did it —
`audit_iteration_summary.json`, `/rounds/0/llm_repair_rationale`:

> *"(2) add NDM-1 as an enzyme to the decomposition reaction **to resolve the structural
> inconsistency where an inhibitor is listed without a target enzyme**."*

`accepted_patch_count: 2, rejected_patch_count: 0`. The row's lineage carrier agrees:

```json
"provenance_lineage": [{"stage": "audit_repair", "origin": "audit_modified",
                        "support": "unsupported", "review_required": false, "sources": []}]
```

**A row with `support: "unsupported"` and `sources: []` was written with `review_required: false`.**

This is PRODUCT_CONTRACT § 1's hard limit hit exactly:

> *"The system must **never invent** reactions, directionality, stoichiometry, identities,
> **enzymes**, locations or other biological content merely to guarantee a PWML file."*

Satisfying a structural-consistency complaint is the schema-shaped form of "merely to guarantee a
PWML file". **The correct repair was to drop the incoherent modifier or flag the row for review —
never to promote the inhibitor to catalyst.**

## 2. Why the policy let it through — the exact hole

`src/t2pw/curation/apply_audit_patch.py::apply_patch_with_policy` (~line 1323) gates an op on:

```python
if confidence < _threshold_for_op(op):          reject
if _is_connectivity_path(path):                 <extra guard>
if enforce_major_topology_threshold and _is_major_topology_path(path):  <extra guard>
if action == "remove" and _is_core_semantics_path(path):                <extra guard>
```

An `add` to `/processes/reactions/N/enzymes/-`:

* is **not** connectivity — `_is_connectivity_path` (line 140) matches only `/inputs` and
  `/outputs`;
* is **not** major topology — `_is_major_topology_path` (line 149) requires the path to end at the
  container or an index;
* is **not** a `remove`.

So it clears on **`confidence >= 0.75` alone** (`_threshold_for_op`, line 169), with **no
requirement that the proposed actor appear in any evidence span**. The module already documents
this exact class of hole at line 814 (*"WHY THE HOLE WAS THERE: `_is_core_semantics_path()` scopes
only to … therefore needed nothing beyond confidence"*). **This is another instance of it, on the
actor-role axis.**

## 4. What to build — general, and never name-specific

**Rule.** An audit patch whose `op` is `add` (or `replace` introducing a new actor) targeting a
process **actor role** — `enzymes`, `modifiers`, `modifiers_or_enzymes`, and the transport
equivalents (`transporters`, `cargo`) on `reactions`, `transports` and
`reaction_coupled_transports` — is **rejected** unless the patch carries an evidence span in which
the proposed actor is named **performing that role**.

**Hard constraints on your implementation:**

* **No paper id, protein name, count or gold value may appear anywhere in production.** The rule is
  about the shape of the patch, not about NDM-1.
* **"Resolving a structural inconsistency" is not an evidence class.** A rationale that argues from
  the payload's own shape rather than from source text must not satisfy the guard.
* An actor already present elsewhere in the payload is **not** thereby evidenced for a **new** role.
  The measured defect is precisely a protein that was legitimately present as an `inhibitor` being
  promoted to `catalyst`. **A guard that accepts "the entity exists in this reaction already" would
  pass the very patch this card exists to reject** — check that yours does not.
* Rejection must be **recorded**, with a distinct reason string, through the existing
  `_patch_log_record` / `rejected_log_records` channel. Silent dropping is not acceptable.
* When the patch is rejected the row must **survive unchanged and remain reviewable** — merge rule
  7. Do not let this guard delete a reaction or demote a leg to `diagnostic_only`.
