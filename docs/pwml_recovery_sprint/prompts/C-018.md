OBJECTIVE
  PRODUCT_CONTRACT section 2 requires, among the correctness conditions:
  "no assay reporters as pathway members" and "no contextual gene-list neighbours
  as participants". The baseline reports 7 unsupported reactions and 10 false
  real identifiers, and part of that population is material that was never a
  pathway participant in the first place -- a reporter used to MEASURE the
  pathway, or a ubiquitous cofactor treated as a first-class member.

  Two existing sites already carry partial, private notions of this:
    src/t2pw/rag/admission.py :: _cofactors (:322), _hubs (:331)
    src/t2pw/bench/semantic.py :: _cofactor_names (:155)
  They disagree, they are not shared, and neither is reusable by the pipeline.

  After this patch: ONE module answers, for a named entity in a stated context,
  whether it is a pathway participant, a ubiquitous cofactor/currency metabolite,
  a hub, or an assay reporter -- with a REASON and a confidence, and with no
  hidden dependence on the caller.

CRITICAL BOUNDARY -- read twice
  This classifier makes an ADVISORY biological judgement. It must never, by
  itself, delete biology. PRODUCT_CONTRACT section 1: "A smaller supported
  pathway is preferable to a larger contaminated one" -- but also, a valid
  pathway core must not be suppressed because peripheral material is unresolved.
  Your module RETURNS A VERDICT. It does not remove anything, and nothing in this
  branch may call it.

  If a classification is uncertain, say so. An "unknown" verdict is a correct
  answer and is far safer than a confident wrong one, because a later branch will
  act on this.

IMPLEMENT
  1. A closed verdict vocabulary: at minimum participant | cofactor_or_currency |
     hub | assay_reporter | unknown. Name them in your report.
  2. Every verdict carries a REASON and an explicit confidence. A bare boolean is
     not reviewable by a biologist, and R-003 is your reviewer.
  3. Context matters and must be a parameter, not an assumption. The same
     molecule can be a currency metabolite in one pathway and the actual product
     in another -- ATP in glycolysis versus ATP synthesis. A context-free
     classifier is wrong by construction; make the requested pathway and organism
     inputs.
  4. Reconcile the three existing private lists (_cofactors, _hubs,
     _cofactor_names) into one source of truth INSIDE your module. Do not edit
     those files -- read them, decide, and document every disagreement you found
     and how you resolved it. That list of disagreements is a required part of
     your report.
  5. Deterministic. No LLM, no network, no database.
  6. Leaf module: no import of pipeline.py, admission.py or streamlit_app.py.

MUST NOT CHANGE
  - rag/admission.py and bench/semantic.py. Read them; do not edit them.
    admission.py is audited as already correct for its job (MASTER_PLAN 2).
  - No caller anywhere. The module must be dead code at merge time, by design.

  b) THE CONTEXT TEST: one molecule classified differently under two different

  c) An ambiguous entity returns unknown rather than guessing.
  d) Determinism: same input, same verdict, across repeated calls.
  e) No network/DB/LLM on any path.
