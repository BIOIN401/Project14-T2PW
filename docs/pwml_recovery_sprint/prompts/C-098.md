## 2. Why this card exists

C-094 stops the PathBank `Unknown` sentinel lending its own organism (*Arabidopsis thaliana*) to the
wrapper built around it. Correct, and measured. But it has a consequence C-094's boundary cannot
address, which two independent measurements established:

**Census over all 92 committed legs** (`evidence/g11/ORCH-706/01`, `/02`):

```
legs carrying an Unknown-backed generated wrapper        : 11
of those, legs that produced a PWML                      :  1
Unknown-backed generated wrappers examined               : 31
  with a correct species surviving underneath the clobber:  6   <- keep their species under C-094
  with NOTHING underneath                                : 25   <- carry no species under C-094
```

A wrapper with nothing underneath fails
`run_strict_post_normalization_gates` (`process_normalizer.py:4661`,
`Generated protein complex '<name>' is missing species/organism.`). That gate has **only**
`_add_error` — every finding is blocking.

**Independent measurement through the production classifier:**

```
strict gates PASS  ->  release_ready     eligible=True   reasons=[]
strict gates FAIL  ->  diagnostic_only   eligible=False  reasons=['strict_technical_gates_blocked_export']
```

and `diagnostic_only` is the no-PWML state (`release_status.py:510-511`, `:719-720`;
`driver.py:1602-1604` `WARN_PWML_WITHHELD_DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`).

**So after C-094, the majority wrapper shape — 25 of 31 — turns a leg that would have produced a
PWML into one that produces none.** It does not manifest on any committed leg only because all 10 of
the other Unknown-backed legs already fail for unrelated reasons. **That is luck, not safety.**

### Why that is a defect and not the desired strictness

* `PRODUCT_CONTRACT` § 1: *"A technically recoverable problem must not cause a run to produce no
  PWML."* Its unacceptable-terminal-blockers list includes **"a valid pathway core suppressed
  because optional peripheral material is unresolved."** The reactions are intact; one placeholder
  actor's organism is unresolved.
* **Merge rule 7**: *"It preserves incomplete-but-correct pathways as `review_required` rather than
  dropping them."*
* `PRODUCT_CONTRACT` § 4: `review_required` is exactly *"valid, useful PWML produced, but one or
  more important biological uncertainties are explicitly identified."* An unresolved organism on a
  placeholder actor is precisely that.
* `PRODUCT_CONTRACT` § 8: *"`not_evaluated` is never treated as `false`."* Unresolved is not wrong.

**This card applies locked rules. It does not make a new product decision.**

## 3. The § 13 consideration — state it, do not resolve it

## 4. Required behaviour — binding

* A generated wrapper whose species is unresolved **and which is explicitly marked as an Unknown-backed
  placeholder** must produce a **`review_required`-grade finding**, not a terminal block.
* **Narrowness is the whole card.** Key the demotion on the marker C-094 records — the
  `mapping_meta.placeholder_record_species` block with `applies_to_entity: false`, and/or the
  `protein_complex_missing_species` issue carrying reason
  `unknown_placeholder_species_not_inherited`. Read C-094's helper
  `_record_placeholder_wrapper_species` for the exact shape.
* **Everything else stays blocking.** A real complex missing species for any other reason, a
  generated wrapper missing species without that marker, a wrapper missing components — all
  unchanged and still terminal. **If your change relaxes any of those, it is a merge-rule-6
  violation and an automatic reject.**
* The finding must remain **visible**: it is a demotion in severity, never a suppression. It must
  still appear in the gate/contract report, and the leg must land on `review_required`, never
  silently on `release_ready`.
* A leg demoted this way must write **`pathway.review_required.pwml`**, never a bare `pathway.pwml`
  (`PRODUCT_CONTRACT` § 13, artifact naming).
* **Do not touch research mode.** `export_mode.relax_report` already demotes strict gate errors
  there. Reusing it wholesale in strict mode would relax every gate and is an automatic reject.

## 5. Seam — determine, justify, then implement

Two candidates. **Decide which, and say why, before writing code:**

* **Seam 1 — the gate.** `run_strict_post_normalization_gates` gains a non-blocking channel for this
  one condition. It currently has only `_add_error`; a `warnings`-equivalent would be new. Narrow in
  condition, but it changes a gate's output shape, and `stage3_gate_report` is consumed in several
  places.
* **Seam 2 — the classification.** The gate keeps emitting its error; the leg's release
  classification recognises that the only blocking findings are marked-placeholder-species ones and
  lands on `review_required` with PWML. Narrow in blast radius on the gate, wider on
  `release_status` / `driver`.

**Report which you chose and why.** If both are needed, that is two cards, not one hidden change —
stop and tell me.
