## 1. The defect

`t2pw.rag.admission.parse_span_relation` returns `None` for the nominalized construction

> `the conversion of chorismate to isochorismate (catalyzed by EntC)`

and for the same shape with the catalyst in a trailing participial phrase

> `The pathway begins with the conversion of chorismate to isochorismate, catalyzed by
> isochorismate synthase (EntC) .`

No template in `_ALL_PROSE_PATTERNS` reads it. `catalyzes_to_subjectless` requires the
governing verb — *"catalyzes the conversion of A to B"* — and these sentences put the
nominalization first and the catalyst after.

The gate therefore refuses these candidates with `evidence_relation_roles_unassignable`,
*"the span states a relation but no implemented pattern could assign
substrate/product/catalyst roles"*. Measured over the committed archive: **294 sole-blocker
rejections of curated-correct chemistry across 12 distinct spans, 71.8% of them this one
construction**, on candidates whose `requested_pathway_match` and `organism_match` are both
`match`, against connectivity gaps they could fill. The reactions lost include
`PMC12452463:R1` / `PMC12096016:R1` — **the first step of the enterobactin pathway**.

   same nominalization.** No catalyst named → the relation carries no catalyst. Never

2. **Both participants come from the span.** Assert only roles the sentence assigns. No
   defaulting and no completing the relation from the claim.
3. **`F-179` is untouchable. REQUIRED NEGATIVE CONTROL:** the template must NOT parse
   `condensation of glycine and succinyl-CoA to produce …`. That is the nominalized-**subject**
   form, it is still a named known false negative in
   `test_the_known_false_negatives_are_named_not_hidden`, and it is the **glycine→heme
   shape** the whole `F-179` repair exists to block. Assert this explicitly in the test
   file. Your template covers the nominalized **`of X to Y`** form with an attached
   catalyst — it must not widen into the subject form.
4. **Negation and hypotheticals must not parse:** `… is not catalyzed by …`,
   `No conversion of … was observed`, `If … were catalyzed by …`.
5. **An anaphoric actor still yields no catalyst** — extend the existing
   `test_an_anaphoric_actor_still_yields_no_catalyst` invariant to this template. `which`
   is not a protein name.
6. `wrong` and `unsupported` in
   `test_recall_over_real_source_sentences_is_measured_and_pinned` **must stay at zero.**
   Recall may go up and `missed` down; those two may not move. Re-pin the numbers you
