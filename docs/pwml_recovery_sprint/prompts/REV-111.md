## The crux

**This card instruments. If it fixes, it has failed.**

The charter's stop conditions are the review's blocking criteria, because the whole value of doing
observability first is lost if a speculative retry change rides along inside it. **You cannot
diagnose a timeout with an instrument that changed the thing it measures.**

## Adversarial work

**B16. Kill the synthetic child in more than one way.** Wall-clock overrun, an exception mid-stage,
and a hard kill with no chance to finalize. **The third is the important one**: if the nine items
only survive a graceful stop, they will not be there the next time a leg is killed the way T-107's
were.

**B17. Confirm "no evidence of retries" is now distinguishable from "no instrument".** That
distinction is the entire reason the card exists — F-148 records retry amplification as
**unexcluded, not excluded**, because *the artifact needed to rule it out is exactly the artifact
the kill destroyed*. After this card, a run with zero retries must be **provably** zero rather than
silent. Note that `LLM_MAX_RETRIES=3` — the mechanism is configured and enabled, so this is not
hypothetical.

**B18. Design a mutation of your own** — remove one of the nine preservations and confirm a test goes
red. Use C-106's `apply_mutation` / `restore_saved_bytes`; **D-084: restores replay SAVED BYTES.**
