## Open questions (for the product owner, after E2E-2)

1. **Per-attempt cap.** `RUN_TIMEOUT_S = 3600` (`web/retry.py:41`) and the 7200 s wall cap (`:47`) are engineering defaults, not recorded decisions. D-W05 says only "a run takes 20-60 minutes". History says 15 of 280 legs ran over 3600 s, and p90 is 43 min.

   | option | what it means | cost |
   |---|---|---|
   | (a) | keep 3600 s | about 5% of legs time out, and are retried once at another hour's cost |
   | (b) | raise to 5400-7200 s and drop timeout from the retry kinds | fewer false failures; a slot is held longer |
   | (c) | decide after E2E-2 shows whether the stall is a bug | none now |

   The orchestrator recommends (c).
2. **Retrying a timeout.** A timed-out run is re-queued for another full attempt. If the cause is a deterministic stall, the retry doubles the cost and still fails.
