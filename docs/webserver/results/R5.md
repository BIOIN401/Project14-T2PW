## 1. What was built

`run_pathway` (`service/pathway.py`) is the headless twin of `batch/driver._drive`: same order and verdict logic, reusing the driver's own helpers through a one-field `at` (`session_state`). The three clicks became the service calls the app makes. The input rules and the result writer are the bridge's (`_unsupported`, `_paper`, `_refuse`, `_to_result`), so job and result pages render as they do today. The bridge is unchanged and selectable with `--run-fn`.

Why it reuses private functions of the driver and the bridge: `batch/driver.py` is outside the file boundary, and copying its 100 lines of verdict logic would add a second source of truth. All imports are inside functions, so importing `pathway.py` stays cheap and acyclic. R6 can invert this.
